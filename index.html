How to play

  • Read the clues. Dark, raised squares are still covered. A number counts all mines touching that square, including diagonals. A blank open square touches zero mines. For example, if a 1 touches an exposed 💣, its other neighbors are safe. A flag is a player’s guess.
  • Open a safe square. In Open mode, click or tap a covered square. A correct move earns 1 point and marks it in your color. The opening clues and any extra squares revealed automatically are shared and worth 0 points.
  • Mark a suspected mine. Right-click, or select ⚑ Flag and tap. Repeat to remove your flag. Only you can see your flags. Each player gets one flag per mine. Flags always earn 0 points.
  • Play around a flag. Remove your flag to open that square yourself. Your opponent can still open it. If either player reveals it, including during an automatic opening, the flag disappears and you can use it again.
  • Hit a mine. You lose 1 point and freeze. Your opponent can keep playing. The exposed 💣 stays visible. The default freeze is 5 seconds.
  • Win the duel. The round ends when every safe square is open or time runs out. The higher score wins. Equal scores tie. On your board, ⚑✓ and ⚑× show which of your remaining flags were right or wrong.
