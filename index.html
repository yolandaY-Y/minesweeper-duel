How to play

  • Open a square. In Open mode, click or tap a raised gray square. Each number tells you how many mines touch it, including diagonally.
  • Follow the blanks. A blank touches zero mines. Opening one also uncovers nearby safe squares.
  • Mark a possible mine. Right-click, or choose Flag and tap. Repeat to remove the flag. Flags are unlimited. To open a flagged square directly, choose Open and tap it.

Solo rules

  • Your first open is safe and starts the clock. Take as long as you like.
  • Open every safe square to win. Opening a mine ends the game immediately.
  • Flags are optional. A blank opens around your flags. In Open mode, tap a flag to uncover that square yourself.

Duel rules

  • Claim squares. Both players see the same nine starting clues. Open a safe square first to claim it in your color and earn 1 point. Automatic openings earn 0.
  • Avoid mines. Opening a mine costs 1 point and freezes you while your opponent keeps playing. The default freeze is 5 seconds.
  • Flag privately. Only you see your flags. They earn 0 points. Either player can open a flagged square, and automatic openings clear flags too.
  • Win on points. When time runs out or all safe squares are open, the higher score wins. Equal scores tie. Remaining flags show ✓ for correct and × for incorrect.
